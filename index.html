Our services

Comprehensive care, one visit at a time.

Every service is performed in-house, with results explained the same day — no long waits for answers.

Digital Colposcopy-Assisted Visual Assessment

VIA with digital colposcopy gives a clear, magnified view of the cervix. Results are instant, so any precancerous changes can be addressed the same visit.

Contraceptive Management

Monthly and 3-monthly injections, IUD placement, and implant placement — tailored to what fits your body and your life.

Treatment of Precancerous Lesions (Thermal Ablation)

Precancerous lesions removed safely and effectively using a heated probe — a quick, well-tolerated procedure done right in clinic.
