Services

Comprehensive care, every step of the way.

From routine screening to same-day treatment, every service below is performed in-house — with clear pricing and no long waits for answers.

Digital Colposcopy-Assisted Visual Assessment

VIA with digital colposcopy allows for better visualization of the cervix and any precancerous lesions present. Results are instantaneous, so action can be taken immediately.

Treatment of Precancerous Lesions (Thermal Ablation)

Digital colposcopy allows visualization of precancerous lesions, which can then be removed with pinpoint accuracy using thermal ablation — preventing progression to cervical cancer.

Pap Smear

Colposcopy isn't suitable for everyone. In cases where it can't be done, a Pap smear is performed instead — a well-established, reliable screening method.

Contraceptive Management

Offered via oral or injectable options depending on what fits best. Every option is discussed thoroughly to make sure the right choice is made for you.

Minor Surgical Procedure

Minor procedures such as mole or skin tag removal are also available. Call to inquire whether the procedure you're interested in can be done here.

General Medical Consultation

Despite offering primarily specialized cervical cancer services, general medical consultation of both adults and children is also available.

*Please note that all services are usually accompanied by a $50 consultation fee.
