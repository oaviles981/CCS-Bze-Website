Who we are

A private screen-and-treat facility, built around one goal.

Cervical Cancer Services Belize is a private screen and treat facility that provides quality, confidential, patient-centered, affordable, and life-saving cervical precancerous lesion treatment.

This unique service is the first of its kind in the Belize District. Our aim is to improve the current cervical cancer screening options in Belize City.

Doing this at scale has proven difficult given the limited human resources available at clinics in Belize, and how, by necessity, those resources need to be allocated. By having a center dedicated exclusively to this goal, we believe a scale can be achieved that has a monumental impact on Belize's population.
